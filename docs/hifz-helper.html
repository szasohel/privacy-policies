Hifz Helper - Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Overview

Hifz Helper ("the App") is a Quran memorization (hifz) tracking and practice companion published by Sanuba Technologies. This policy explains what information the App handles and how it is used. We built the App to work entirely without accounts, ads, or analytics, and this policy reflects that.

Information We Collect

The App does not require you to create an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information. There are no login screens, ads, or analytics/tracking SDKs built into the App.

Data Stored On Your Device

To provide memorization scheduling and progress tracking, the App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Your memorization progress, tasks, and completion logs (e.g. which surahs/ayahs you have reviewed).
  • App preferences, such as your chosen reciter and reading settings.
  • Downloaded Quran audio files cached locally so you can replay recitations without re-downloading them.

This data never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or any third party, and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the App.

Third-Party Services

The App fetches Quran text, translations, and audio recitations from the following third-party services. Only the Quran reference you request (surah number, ayah number, or reciter selection) is sent in these requests - no personal information is included:

  • ummahapi.com - provides Quran verse text and translations.
  • everyayah.com - provides ayah-by-ayah audio recitations.

These services are operated independently of Sanuba Technologies and are not covered by this policy. We encourage you to review their own privacy practices if you have concerns.

Permissions

The App requests internet access solely to download the Quran text, translations, and audio described above. It does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, or location.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, and is safe for use by learners of all ages.

Data Sharing & Sale

We do not sell, rent, or share any data, because no personal data is collected from you off of your device in the first place.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date above.
